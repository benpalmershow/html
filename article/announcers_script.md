# The Big Game — Halftime Show

## Characters

- **DALE RIGGS** — Color commentator. Mid-50s. Wearing a headset two sizes too small. Currently unraveling.
- **CHIP WAINWRIGHT** — Play-by-play. Chipper. Impeccably decorous. Has never said anything stronger than "golly" on air.

*(They are in a broadcast booth overlooking a football field. Crowd noise below. A marching band is tuning up. DALE is eating a hot dog with both hands. CHIP is adjusting his tie.)*

## Scene

**CHIP**
And we're back here at Crisler Center for the second half of this Gridiron Classic, and Dale, what a first half we've seen — sixteen penalties, three fumbles, and a marching band tuba player who appears to have gone rogue near the twenty-yard line.

**DALE**
*(mouth full)*
Mmph. Yeah. Chip. Buddy. Pal. So my wife — last night — she had the shampoo bottle. And she started tickling me. Right here. *(points to his ribs)* Under the arms. Then she put it somewhere else. Somewhere it doesn't belong in a marriage. And I said, "Honey, that's the spot," and she said, "I know, Dale," and then things got... intense.

**CHIP**
*(without missing a beat)*
And the home team comes out in a nickel package, looking to establish the run early—

**DALE**
Intense, Chip. She sat on my face. I couldn't breathe. I was seeing stars. She said, "Tap out," and I said, "Never, Chip. Never tap out."

**CHIP**
...and we'll see if the offensive line can hold up against that aggressive front four.

**DALE**
Then she got on top of me and rode me like she was trying to break the headboard. I was staring at the ceiling thinking about property taxes while she bounced. I was thinking about mu 7% mortgage. 

**CHIP**
*(checking his notes)*
The visitors have only managed sixty-two yards of total offense, which is well below their season average. Dale, any thoughts on their quarterback's pocket presence?

**DALE**
She had me pinned down. I couldn't move my arms. She was using both hands — one here, one there — and I'm just laying there going, "Chip, somebody help me. Somebody help me."

**CHIP**
Right. And speaking of streaks, the home team is on a three-game losing streak, so there's a lot of pressure on their coach to—

**DALE**
Then she wanted to do it in the butt. And I said, "Honey, we discussed this," and she said, "We discussed it, but I changed my mind," and I said, "Well I didn't change mine," and she said, "You don't have a choice," and then she did it anyway. She just did it. And I yelled. I yelled so loud the neighbors banged on the wall.

**CHIP**
*(sipping coffee)*
Dale, if you're seeing this, the tight end is running a post route across the middle—

**DALE**
She finished.

**CHIP**
Dale.

**DALE**
—anyway, then she wanted to take it slow. Just lie there. Don't let anything get too deep. Play it safe. So she got on top and just... floated. Very gentle. Very careful. I was open the whole time but she wouldn't move.

**CHIP**
*(into headset)*
And the halftime show is about to begin, folks. We'll be back after these messages.

**DALE**
Chip. Chip, we haven't even gotten to the quickie.

**CHIP**
We're going to break now, Dale.

**DALE**
Quickie, Chip. Fast. No foreplay. Just pumping away like a rabbit.

**CHIP**
Dale, we are off the air.

**DALE**
—off the air?! Chip, I haven't even described the glory hole in the guest bathroom yet.

**CHIP**
*(standing up, walking toward the booth door)*
I'm going to go find the producer.

**DALE**
*(calling after him)*
It's not what it sounds like! It's just a hole! For glory! And also my wife was there!

*(CHIP exits. DALE puts his headset back on, leans into the mic.)*

**DALE**
And we're back. My wife wants me to wear the nurse outfit again tonight.

*(Blackout.)*
