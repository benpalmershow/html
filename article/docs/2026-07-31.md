## Housing & Rates

[30-yr Mortgage Rate](financials.html?filter=latest&indicator=30-yr%20Mortgage%20Rate): 6.66% (Jun) vs 6.44% (May), MoM +3.42%, YoY -2.34% vs 2025 Jun. Rates drifted higher despite the FOMC holding at the July meeting, tracking the 10yr Treasury selloff. Refinance demand remains suppressed.

[Housing Starts](financials.html?filter=latest&indicator=Housing%20Starts): 1.427M (Jun) vs 1.199M (May), MoM +19.0%. Builders pushed ahead despite elevated borrowing costs. Single-family and multifamily both rose, suggesting inventory-pipeline commitments are keeping crews busy.

[Pending Home Sales](financials.html?filter=latest&indicator=Pending%20Home%20Sales%20Index): 72.5 (Jun) vs 76.6 (May), MoM -5.35%. Contract signings cooled as affordability remains stretched and inventory turnover slows. A leading indicator for existing sales.

[Used Vehicle Values](financials.html?filter=latest&indicator=Used%20Vehicle%20Value%20Index): 211.5 (Jul) vs 213.9 (Jun), MoM -1.1%. Wholesale auction prices normalizing from 2026 peak. Downside bias for used car inflation.

[Case-Shiller National Home Price Index](financials.html?filter=latest&indicator=Case-Shiller%20National%20Home%20Price%20Index): 335.104 (May) vs 332.984 (Apr), MoM +0.64%. Home prices continue grinding higher despite affordability headwinds. Wealth effect intact.

## Rates & Currency

[10yr Treasury Yield](financials.html?filter=latest&indicator=10-yr%20Treasury%20Yield): 4.67% (Jul) vs 4.48% (Jun), MoM +4.24%. Long-end yields climbing as market prices in persistent inflation and expanding fiscal supply. The Fed's credibility on hold is not enough to anchor duration.

[Dollar Index](financials.html?filter=latest&indicator=Dollar%20Value%20Index): 120.52 (Jul) vs 120.97 (Jun), MoM -0.37%. Broad dollar easing slightly from recent highs but still in a strong range. Export competitiveness remains under pressure.


## Labor

[Real-Time Unemployment Forecast](financials.html?filter=latest&indicator=Real-Time%20Unemployment%20Rate%20Forecast): 4.13% (Jul) vs 4.19% (Jun), MoM -1.43%. Chicago Fed high-frequency estimate points to continued labor market cooling, consistent with gradual payroll deceleration.

[Private Employment](financials.html?filter=latest&indicator=Private%20Employment): 132,722K (Jun) vs 132,624K (May), MoM +98K. Hiring running above replacement rate but below 2026 boom pace. Net-net steady.

## Trade & Energy

[Strait of Hormuz Daily Transits](financials.html?filter=latest&indicator=Strait%20of%20Hormuz%20Daily%20Transits): 11 (Jul) vs 3 (Jun), MoM +267%. Shipping volume stepped up from June trough but remains far below pre-crisis 100-138/day. Vessel risk premium still elevated.

[Personal Consumption Expenditures](financials.html?filter=latest&indicator=Personal%20Consumption%20Expenditures%20%28PCE%29): $22,184.1B (Jun) vs $22,118.9B (May), MoM +0.29%. Consumer spending grinding higher despite elevated rates. Income effects from fiscal stimulus still supporting demand.

[Retail Sales](financials.html?filter=latest&indicator=Monthly%20Retail%20Sales): $768.6B (Jun) vs $766.9B (May), MoM +0.22%. Core retail holding firm. Consumer resilient but not robust. Service spending continues outpacing goods.

## Government

[Treasury Debt Level](financials.html?filter=latest&indicator=Treasury%20Debt%20Level): $39.80T (Jul) vs $39.21T (Jun), MoM +1.49%. Debt accumulation accelerating toward $40T milestone. Interest on debt running ~$92B/mo crowding out productive capital.

## Manufacturing

[Manufacturing PMI](financials.html?filter=latest&indicator=Manufacturing%20PMI): 53.3 (Jun) vs 54.0 (May), MoM -1.30%. Still in expansion territory (>50) but momentum easing. New orders sub-index likely softening.

[Consumer Confidence](financials.html?filter=latest&indicator=Consumer%20Confidence): 90.8 (Jul) vs 92.2 (Jun), MoM -1.52%. Present situation and expectations both down. Consumers feeling pinch from rates and energy prices.
