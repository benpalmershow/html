## Housing & Rates

30-yr Mortgage Rate: 6.66% (Jun) vs 6.44% (May), MoM +3.42%, YoY -2.34% vs 2025 Jun. Rates drifted higher despite the FOMC holding at the July meeting, tracking the 10yr Treasury selloff. Refinance demand remains suppressed.

Housing Starts: 1.427M (Jun) vs 1.199M (May), MoM +19.0%. Builders pushed ahead despite elevated borrowing costs. Single-family and multifamily both rose, suggesting inventory-pipeline commitments are keeping crews busy.

Pending Home Sales: 72.5 (Jun) vs 76.6 (May), MoM -5.35%. Contract signings cooled as affordability remains stretched and inventory turnover slows. A leading indicator for existing sales.

Used Vehicle Values: 211.5 (Jul) vs 213.9 (Jun), MoM -1.1%. Wholesale auction prices normalizing from 2026 peak. Downside bias for used car inflation.

Case-Shiller National Home Price Index: 335.104 (May) vs 332.984 (Apr), MoM +0.64%. Home prices continue grinding higher despite affordability headwinds. Wealth effect intact.

## Rates & Currency

10yr Treasury Yield: 4.67% (Jul) vs 4.48% (Jun), MoM +4.24%. Long-end yields climbing as market prices in persistent inflation and expanding fiscal supply. The Fed's credibility on hold is not enough to anchor duration.

Dollar Index: 120.52 (Jul) vs 120.97 (Jun), MoM -0.37%. Broad dollar easing slightly from recent highs but still in a strong range. Export competitiveness remains under pressure.


## Labor

Real-Time Unemployment Forecast: 4.13% (Jul) vs 4.19% (Jun), MoM -1.43%. Chicago Fed high-frequency estimate points to continued labor market cooling, consistent with gradual payroll deceleration.

Private Employment: 132,722K (Jun) vs 132,624K (May), MoM +98K. Hiring running above replacement rate but below 2026 boom pace. Net-net steady.

## Trade & Energy

Strait of Hormuz Daily Transits: 11 (Jul) vs 3 (Jun), MoM +267%. Shipping volume stepped up from June trough but remains far below pre-crisis 100-138/day. Vessel risk premium still elevated.

Personal Consumption Expenditures: $22,184.1B (Jun) vs $22,118.9B (May), MoM +0.29%. Consumer spending grinding higher despite elevated rates. Income effects from fiscal stimulus still supporting demand.

Retail Sales: $768.6B (Jun) vs $766.9B (May), MoM +0.22%. Core retail holding firm. Consumer resilient but not robust. Service spending continues outpacing goods.

## Government

Treasury Debt Level: $39.80T (Jul) vs $39.21T (Jun), MoM +1.49%. Debt accumulation accelerating toward $40T milestone. Interest on debt running ~$92B/mo crowding out productive capital.

## Manufacturing

Manufacturing PMI: 53.3 (Jun) vs 54.0 (May), MoM -1.30%. Still in expansion territory (>50) but momentum easing. New orders sub-index likely softening.

Consumer Confidence: 90.8 (Jul) vs 92.2 (Jun), MoM -1.52%. Present situation and expectations both down. Consumers feeling pinch from rates and energy prices.
