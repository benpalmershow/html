**Filed:** August 3, 2026

# Executive Summary

Robinhood Ventures Fund II is registering an interval fund that provides retail investors access to a diversified portfolio of venture capital investments that have traditionally been available primarily to institutional and accredited investors.

This filing is an amendment (N-2/A) updating the registration statement prior to the fund becoming effective.
