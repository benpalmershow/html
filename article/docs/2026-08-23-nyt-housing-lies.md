> *Two irreconcilable visions. The street versus the book. Reality v. theory.*

You cannot camp on the street in a civilized society. **Rule-based order** is the foundation of prosperity, and society's performative empathy — "pretending to care" about trauma and abandonment — is nothing short of *oxymoronic and terrifying*. | Homelessness is **not a moral failure**. It is a *complex collision* of mental health crises, addiction, domestic violence, medical debt, and a catastrophic shortage of affordable housing. Criminalizing camping does not end suffering — it **drives it underground**, cycling people through jails and emergency rooms at **staggering public cost**. |

The answer to underfunding and NIMBY resistance is **reform and investment**, not abolition. Blaming generational cohorts distracts from the real engines of unaffordability: **zoning restrictions, corporate landlord consolidation, and stagnant wages**. |
