## Housing: Remove Impediments

[Housing Starts](financials.html?filter=latest&indicator=Housing%20Starts) rebound when the [inclusionary zoning](https://www.nhcus.org/resource/inclusionary-zoning-fact-sheet) ratio drops below 20%. At 5% - the sweet spot - developers actually break ground. The math is simple: 20% mandatory affordable set-aside kills the deal. 5% keeps it alive.

[Housing Permit](financials.html?filter=latest&indicator=Housing%20Starts) activity tracks the [30-yr Mortgage Rate](financials.html?filter=latest&indicator=30-yr%20Mortgage%20Rate) and the [Affordability Index](financials.html?filter=latest&indicator=Affordability%20Index) almost tick-for-tick. When rates drift above 6.5% and affordability falls below 100, permitting halts. When rates retreat and affordability recovers, foundations rise.

**The recipe is not more subsidies.** Higher incentives get people fucking rich. Lower the onerous requirements installed by old people (government). Increase the number of buildings. Get out the fucking way, boomer.

| Policy Variable | Threshold | Effect |
|-----------------|-----------|--------|
| Inclusionary set-aside | 20% | Project uneconomic |
| Inclusionary set-aside | 5% | Project viable |
| 30-yr mortgage rate | >6.5% | Permits slump |
| 30-yr mortgage rate | <6.0% | Permits recover |
| Affordability Index | <100 | Demand destroyed |
| Affordability Index | >120 | Demand revives |
