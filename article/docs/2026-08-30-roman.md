### Webb

Launched in 2021, JWST is the largest and most powerful space telescope ever built. Operating primarily in infrared, it peers through dust clouds and sees the first galaxies forming.

### Hubble

Launched in 1990, the Hubble Space Telescope was the first major optical telescope in space. It has provided iconic images of the cosmos and fundamentally changed our understanding of the universe.

- [Hubble Overview - NASA](https://www.nasa.gov/hubble)
- [Hubble Images - ESA/Hubble](https://esahubble.org/)

### What Makes Each Unique

- **Hubble** (1990) operates from Low Earth Orbit and spent 34+ years capturing the first visible-light image of an exoplanet, Fomalhaut b, and its Ultra Deep Field revealed thousands of unseen galaxies from just 10 days of exposure.
- **Webb** (2021) sits at Sun-Earth L2, 932,000 miles away, at -391°F, and used its 21.3 ft mirror to detect carbon dioxide in an exoplanet atmosphere and spot galaxies at redshifts z~13-16, challenging models of early universe formation.
- **Roman** (2026) shares Webb's Sun-Earth L2 orbit but covers a field of view 100 times wider than Hubble's, using its wide-field camera for months-long dark-energy surveys and a coronagraph to directly image exoplanets by blocking starlight.
