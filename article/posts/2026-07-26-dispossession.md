There’s another word for dispossession. It’s conquest. And its story is as old as time. And to be honest, it’s a good story (since we have to moralize everything in order to appease the victim trolls who will inevitably shame us).

We can be empathetic, but it’s sometimes it’s refreshing to see the honest violence in Braveheart. Some slits your girl, you slit them back. And then you pound their head in. You bludgeon them. Sometimes you need to beat the shit out of people. That’s what we did before iPhones.

Here’s someone who has no protected status - the Troll. Not the keyboard one with quick fingers and ample time. The real one. The ugly one. The one with hair on its knuckles and an intimidating scowl who lives under a bridge. That one!

The billy goat’s Gruff fend off the Troll because he has the audacity to claim title to the bridge. Whose bridge? The eldest goat is William Wallace with a horns sheathed in keratin. No thank you. No land acknowledgment necessary.

Hollywood has become dispossessed. What used to be theirs is someone else’s. Antitrust M&A stole their industry. They claimed title to the titles of novels, magazines, television, movies. Now TikTok gives title to whomever wants to hit record on their iPhone. Hollywood’s land was stolen because big tech conquered them.

Dispossession is just the other side of the story. It’s the luddite versus a datacenter. The story we like to see is conquest. Who won? Who fought back? Who successfully appealed and righted a wrong. It’s up to indigenous to take back their land just like it’s up to the settlers in the West Bank to claim theirs. Everyone is fighting to survive. One day our only possession will die.
