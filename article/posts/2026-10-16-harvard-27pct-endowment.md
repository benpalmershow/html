## The NYT's Gloating Misses the Story

The New York Times: *"As Trump Cuts, Harvard's Coffer Gushes to $68 Billion."* The framing is pure Trump-defiant triumphalism — the bad guy tried to hurt us, but we outsmarted him. That's the kind of journalism people accept without asking questions.

But the real question is: **how did Harvard earn a 27% return?**

## The Answer Is in the 13F

Harvard Management Company filed its Q2 2026 13F on August 14, covering holdings as of June 30, 2026. The portfolio totals **$4.26 billion** across 19 positions. And at the top of that list, representing **51.8% of the entire portfolio**, is a single position:

> **SPACE EXPLORATION TECHN CORP (SPACEX)** — $2,210,091,186

That's over two billion dollars in a company run by Trump's bestie. The NYT's "gloat" is that Harvard stood tall against federal pressure — but the actual engine of that 27% return wasn't heroic institutional resilience. It was a single, massive, un diversified bet on SpaceX.
