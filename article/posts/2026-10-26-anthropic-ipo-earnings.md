Anthropic, PBC filed its S-1 registration statement with the SEC on October 26, 2026, disclosing the first public financial figures for the Claude AI developer ahead of its planned Nasdaq listing under the ticker ANT.

Revenue reached $16.3 billion in the six months ended June 30, 2026, up from $1.16 billion in the year-earlier period. Full-year 2025 revenue was $4.6 billion, following $386 million in 2024 and $41 million in 2023. Quarterly revenue climbed from $4.7 billion in Q1 2026 to $11.6 billion in Q2 2026, and Anthropic separately told prospective investors that Q3 revenue reached $16.8 billion.

Operating expenses for H1 2026 totaled $17.0 billion, driven by $8.9 billion in compute and infrastructure costs, $3.2 billion in personnel, and $3.2 billion in sales, marketing, and partnerships. Operating income was a loss of $693 million in H1 2026, compared with a $2.1 billion loss in H1 2025, though Q2 2026 produced $974 million in operating income on its own.

The headline net loss of $80.7 billion for H1 2026 is almost entirely an accounting artifact: $72.4 billion stems from changes in the fair value of convertible notes and other financing instruments as Anthropic's enterprise valuation rose. Excluding stock-based compensation and noncash charitable contributions, adjusted operating income was $1.62 billion for the period. Operating cash flow was positive at $3.93 billion, and the company reported $1.70 of revenue for every dollar spent on computing power in Q3.

As of September 30, more than 1,900 customers generated at least $1 million each in annualized revenue, including roughly 250 that generated at least $10 million. Anthropic held $131.7 billion in cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments as of June 30, 2026.

The IPO price range and share count have not yet been determined. Anthropic previously raised a $65 billion Series H round in May 2026 that valued the company at $965 billion, following a $30 billion round in February that valued it at $380 billion.
