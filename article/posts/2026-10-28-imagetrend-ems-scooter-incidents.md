[EMS electric scooter incidents](https://www.imagetrend.com/research-reports/ems-electric-scooter-incidents/) analyzes real EMS data to understand injury patterns from shared e-scooters. The research examines incident types, injury severity, time of day, location patterns, and demographics. 

As micromobility has expanded rapidly, this data helps quantify the public safety impact and informs discussions around speed limits, helmet use, where scooters can operate, and infrastructure needs.
