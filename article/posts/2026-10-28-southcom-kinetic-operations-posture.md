Since September 2025, U.S. forces under SOUTHCOM have conducted more than 70 lethal kinetic strikes against vessels suspected of drug trafficking in the Caribbean and Eastern Pacific. These operations have resulted in over 240 deaths of individuals aboard those vessels. The strikes are part of an intensified counternarcotics campaign targeting narco-trafficking networks operating in the region.

SOUTHCOM regularly publishes press releases on operations, including [lethal kinetic strikes](https://www.southcom.mil/News/PressReleases/Article/4634591/lethal-kinetic-strike-october-27-2026/). The [2026 Posture Statement to Congress](https://www.southcom.mil/Media/Special-Coverage/SOUTHCOMs-2026-Posture-Statement-to-Congress/) details the command's priorities, challenges, and resource needs in the region.
