[Lombard credit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lombard_credit) is a secured loan where a borrower pledges assets like securities as collateral to obtain short-term financing. Originating from Lombard Street in London, these loans allow investors to access liquidity without liquidating their positions. Interest rates and loan terms depend on the collateral's quality and market conditions.
