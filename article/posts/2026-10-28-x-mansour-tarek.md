Tarek Mansour, CEO of [Kalshi](https://kalshi.com/), argues that prediction markets are fundamentally different from sportsbooks. 

A sportsbook sets the odds and takes the other side of your bet, while prediction markets are actual markets where buyers and sellers compete to discover and produce efficient prices. This structure enables important public benefits by aggregating information and revealing collective expectations about future events.
