BYD's new second-generation Seagull EV is now its cheapest electric car in China, going on sale with 5-minute Flash Charging and a starting price of 62,900 yuan (about $9,500) under the Super Trade-in promotion. Regular pricing starts at 65,900 yuan ($9,900), below the first-generation Seagull's 69,900 yuan ($10,500) without incentives. Five variants span 65,900 to 99,900 yuan ($9,900 to $15,000).

The hatchback grows 425 mm longer with a 150 mm longer wheelbase, now measuring 4,205 mm long, 1,810 mm wide, 1,570 mm high, with a 2,650 mm wheelbase and room for five passengers. It switches from front-wheel drive to rear-wheel drive, using an 85 kW (114 hp) or 95 kW (127 hp) motor up from the predecessor's 55 kW (75 hp), with a multi-link independent rear suspension.

BYD Blade Battery 2.0 packs come in 30.1 kWh and 39.2 kWh sizes, delivering CLTC ranges of 320 km (199 miles) and 420 km (261 miles). Flash Charging takes the battery from 10% to 70% in five minutes, 10% to 97% in nine minutes, and 20% to 97% in 12 minutes even at -30°C.

Inside, a 15.6-inch center screen, DiLink 150 smart cockpit, two-spoke steering wheel, and optional DiPilot 300 with God's Eye B (roof-mounted LiDAR, City Navigation on Autopilot, smart parking). Front seats gain heating, ventilation, and massage, plus ambient lighting and a 50W wireless charger.

The Seagull is sold as the Dolphin Surf and Dolphin Mini in Europe, the UK, and other global markets, with the larger upgraded version expected overseas after the China launch.
