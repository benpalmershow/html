I love severe weather. I like when Mother Nature destroys. It teaches me humility and respect for nature. It reminds me of my mortality. It makes me feel alive when I survive a bruising bout with my uninvited finality. Sailing reminds me the ocean will swallow you up and not care an iota. Getting home is triumphant. Being with loved ones between adventures is life.

A heavy rain is violent but not angry. White water will suck you under and pull you to your death. So it's only natural that severe weather is antagonizing and that we may be inclined to moralize it since we could one day die from it, and that matters. It matters that we survive and help others to survive too. If Christmas brings us tropical fish, we should try to forecast our next spiritual aquatic communion. Save us all, dear God!

So when we write about the weather, sunshine is friendly, heavy rain is mean. And El Niño is a "chaos agent." But of course chaos is life and we are agents. Life is agentic chaos. Bad things and good things will always happen. American hubris is convinced we can make only good things happen. We can flatten the curve, we can stop global warming.

The American meteorologist is always the good guy. She is there to warn and save you. And without forecasts of course, we would have no industry, no reliable prediction for estimating functioning futures. Capital markets rely on certainty.

But by inducing emotional responses in forecasting probabilities, we have made weather catastrophic instead of just reality. Kalshi and Polymarket agnostically predict what most people think will happen instead of what one angry evangelist thinks will happen. A waterfall is a difference in elevation. At the bottom of Niagara Falls is chaos or water? A big storm is always on its way. A big bad storm is a disaster with insurance claims. But a big storm is also a difference in atmospheric pressure.

So we humanize weather and make the climate have feelings. Remember the climate is warming because we have been naughty. It will cool, if we only would stop being selfish and mean!
The weather is enthusiastic and collaborative. Be nice, guys!

> "But this time, the atmosphere is collaborating really, really well," said Wenju Cai, a climate scientist at the Ocean University of China. "It's responding to the ocean very, very enthusiastically."

Climate science is serious stuff. For nerds who love fear-mongering and have little appetite for risk. For bookworms who loathe adventure and cannot accept life's uncertainty. For safety lovers, and virtuous snobs, life is to be controlled. There is always a formidable threat to fight, an existential risk to avert, and only with the power of mobilized voters can we demand more government outlays to bring us joy and fulfillment. Remember COVID. They told you to stay home because it was too dangerous to leave. They destroyed economies to flatten a curve that did not exist. But COVID was only trying to demonstrate that Octogenarians are mortal.

We need a tornado watch for inept authority. When a police officer comes after you and your surfboard, it is not for your own good. You need Baywatch and a boner, not onerous rules and infertility. You should be warned of American bureaucracy fighting Mother Nature. When they try to block the sun, I will have told you so. Mob ethics will destroy the Buffalo in the name of domestication and predictability for the public good.

Only the US government can prevent disasters from causing catastrophic impending doom! We must build walls to prevent flooding; moats around our futile sand castles.

> "Funding cutbacks from nations like the United States have left holes in the global system for preventing disasters and responding to them."

Holes in systems. No prevention of disasters. Funding cuts for billionaires. Greedy fossil fuel and insurance companies. Evil White Men. They don't care about you drowning, burning, or dying. We must tax to survive. Only FEMA and building codes can save you!

> "This El Niño could cause nearly 50 million more people across the globe to experience acute hunger, an increase of roughly a fifth, according to the United Nations World Food Program."

Climate kills. And if you are poor, it may kill you sooner. If you are in low elevations, flooding will inevitably impact you. If you are on a Learjet in a high elevation storm, your plane will get iced and go down. If you are a liberal on the coast, your house could become submerged. If you are a conservative in arid tree-country, your house may burn. But none of it can be blamed on Mother Nature because it doesn't matter to Her. She is wholly indifferent to your suffering. And I love that.
