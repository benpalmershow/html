title: "October CPI +0.4%, 86K Jobs, $334B Household Debt"
---

<p>The [Consumer Price Index](financials.html?indicator=CPI) rose a seasonally adjusted 0.4% in October, ahead of the 0.3% that economists expected. September was revised to 0.6%. The Bureau of Labor Statistics could not publish a year over year figure because last year's government shutdown prevented October 2025 data collection. Shelter accounted for more than 30% of the monthly increase, and energy rose 0.7% after a 5.2% jump in September.</p>

<p>Private sector employers added 86,000 jobs in October, per the [ADP National Employment Report](financials.html?indicator=Private%20Employment), matching the revised September total. Trade, transportation, and utilities led with 39,000, while professional and business services shed 27,000. Base pay rose 3.2% year over year.</p>

<p>The [NFIB Small Business Optimism Index](financials.html?indicator=Small%20Business%20Optimism%20Index) edged down to 97.8 from 97.9, the third consecutive monthly decline and the lowest reading since June. The Uncertainty Index fell six points to 84, the lowest since December 2025. Taxes jumped ten points as the second most cited problem, the highest since May 2021.</p>

<p>The [New York Fed Q3 Household Debt and Credit Report](financials.html?indicator=Total%20Household%20Debt) showed total borrowing expanded by $334 billion to $19.105 trillion, led by a $257 billion mortgage increase. Credit card balances hit a record $1.285 trillion. The aggregate delinquency rate rose to 5.0%.</p>

<p>The [Johnson Redbook Retail Sales](financials.html?indicator=Johnson%20Redbook%20Retail%20Sales) index came in at 10.1% year over year for the week ending November 7, down from 10.4% but well above the 9.3% consensus. The [November WASDE report](financials.html?indicator=WASDE%20Supply%20%26%20Demand%20Estimates) lowered corn ending stocks to 1,758 million bushels and soybean ending stocks to 300 million bushels, while raising wheat to 743 million.</p>
