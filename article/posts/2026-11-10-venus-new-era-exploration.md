Venus is shrouded in thick clouds of sulfuric acid that reflect most incoming sunlight, keeping the surface in dim, orange-hued light. Below that haze, atmospheric pressure is 90 times Earth's and surface temperatures reach 864°F (462°C) — hot enough to melt lead. DAVINCI's descent probe will sample the chemistry of these clouds as it falls, measuring how sulfuric acid breaks down at different altitudes to help explain where the planet's water went.
