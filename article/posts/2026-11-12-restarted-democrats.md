> "This country can overcome smallness and cruelty if leaders include everyone." — Ruben Gallego

Gallego demands a $20 min wage for everyone so workers can fill up their big ass truck. Cool. If it works. But Trump has that card. He just blew it in the end. That’s all Dems got. His mistake.

A $20 federal minimum wage, child tax credit, universal healthcare and a “massive housing plan” targeting first-time home buyers won’t do it, unfortunately. The housing plan is conveniently quantified but more importantly, it needs to be qualified and detailed. What is massive? Down payments for 50%-AMI families? You'll need to increase debt service for the $93B monthly interest on debt and push rates up even higher. Your 30yr fixed at +7% absorbs all that massive stimulus.

The Fight for the people needs to be effective. You can’t just throw random punches.

Here are some effective punches.

- Don’t tell voters they need to buy a big truck. Don’t tell them they need to buy a Prius.

A KEI might be a better use of $60,000; the surplus in a restricted index fund earning more than the 10 yr. Let them decide. Education for individual economic actors.

- Let employers decide on wages.

Don’t give out money for child care, healthcare, or housing. The private market is your starting point. Prevent price distortion and facilitate efficient markets (Mamdani caught DoorDash - Copy and Paste). Enforce anti-trust. Regulate. 

- Tax effectively, not punitively.

- Give voters unfettered access to AI.

- Let the market make everything cheap, not the government. Go do Cuba for cheap electricity that never works.

Oh, and if you mean everyone, you’ll have to continue deportations for that utopian EVERYONE to be feasible.
