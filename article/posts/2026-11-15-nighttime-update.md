Our home router updates at 3am every night. Beyond the walls, it may even rely on legacy analog copper wire. Our internet never goes down because the internet is essential to our family.

We have a household mandate, funds have been allocated, and the private market has been a great partner so far. We've appropriated $50/mo. and everything has been just fine. We have cellular backups. We take our connectivity seriously.

We can assume that L3Harris takes the nationwide ground stop and its impact on travelers, airlines and air traffic operations seriously. Why then do they need to make a statement saying so? It's implied. No need for the statement. Just tell us why you upgraded an analog copper wire to a modern fiber optic cable at 3pm and not 3am? Was there a data flood risk analysis done because it was remote and routine? Someone said go ahead and that someone should be fired. Presumptions are obviously dangerous in aviation. (See previous [No Transponder](/journal.html#no-transponder) article) That's why we enable robust inference and abide by tedious workflows. WAYMO doesn't rely on humans for evidence and reasoning to make split-case decisions. The skill for the prompt was already there; the pre planning to the execution. They have backup to the backup; an intricate and robust inference menu ready to be ordered. Pilots are trained to make planned decisions. Why not IT?
