### Oil Me Up 

The NYT interviews a playwright to expound on the "unlimited pillage" of Venezuelan oil by Evil Orange. But the pillage of course was at the behest of their Far Left government.

>"Even today, oil permeates everything from rap lyrics to the memes Venezuelans share on their phones."

Oil is an essential commodity, not some abstract cultural phenomenon to be analyzed through the lens of an aloof ethnographer. They are reporting how people view oil.

 People don't view oil. They buy it. To heat their house or to move around. It's like water. We have poems and rap lyrics about water too. If energy is not cheap, voters rebel. That's all government really needs to do (see Iran) - Facilitate cheap and abundant energy and let competing private self interests harness human cognition. Voters demand cheap energy, not a strongman.
