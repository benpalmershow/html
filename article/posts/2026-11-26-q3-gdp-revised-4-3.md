### <i data-lucide='trending-up' class='post-icon'></i> **Q3 GDP Revised Up to 4.3%**

The Commerce Department's Bureau of Economic Analysis revised third-quarter real GDP growth up to a 4.3% annualized rate in its second estimate, up sharply from the 3.4% advance estimate reported last month. The 0.9 percentage point upward revision was driven mainly by stronger investment and consumer spending.

{{chart:GDP}}

The acceleration from the second quarter's 2.2% pace marks the fastest growth in three years. Real final sales to private domestic purchasers rose 5.8%, also revised up 0.9 point from the prior estimate. The price index for gross domestic purchases increased 2.7%, revised down 0.3 point.

The GDP indicator now tracks the real (inflation-adjusted) quarter-over-quarter growth rate at a seasonally adjusted annual rate, rather than nominal dollar levels.
