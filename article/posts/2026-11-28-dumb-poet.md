To be a poet  
It’s not too late  
I’ve been told  
There’s no time  
To contemplate  

Lying lyrics  
Paycheck fearless  
Dumb money mortgage  
Smart no debt  
Dumb dollars doing  
Smart love musing  

Dumb people losing  
Smart business cruising  
Dumb time undoing  
Smart efficiency productively moving
  
Up and down  
Safe and sound  
Dumb de dumb dumb dumb. (How dare you suggest one dumb)
