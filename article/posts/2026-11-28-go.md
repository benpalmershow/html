# Go (expounded)

Bright lights  
Silent gas station  
Credit card inserted  
Pump gas at 87  
Under $100, used to be over  
Dump the handle on the lever  
Click no thanks  
Reset the mileage to 0  
Turn the wheel  
get on the road  
The car is purring  
a full tank  
The young ones want to know  
are we there yet?  
In the mind, the credit card balance  
When at home, login, reminder  
Hoping no one bumps your car  
Every ride is a bet  
insurance premiums ready to set
